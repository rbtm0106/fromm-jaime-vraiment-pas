# Fromm j'aime vraiment pas!

A client-side web player to stream Fromm content in your browser.

👉 **[Open the Web Player](https://rbtm0106.github.io/fromm-jaime-vraiment-pas/)**

---

## Setup

Because the player runs in your browser, you need a local extension to bypass cors restrictions for the video stream.

### 1. Install the Extension

**Firefox:**
* Install from [Mozilla Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/addon/fromm-j-aime-vraiment-pas/).

**Chrome / Brave / Edge:**
1. Download [`fromm_extension.zip`](https://github.com/rbtm0106/fromm-jaime-vraiment-pas/releases/latest/download/fromm_extension.zip) and extract the folder.
2. Go to `chrome://extensions/` and enable **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the extracted folder.
*(Video guide for older version: [YouTube](https://youtu.be/9MmJEB1P_sg))*

*(For other browsers, you can feed the 2 files from the Chrome `.zip` to an LLM to port it).*

### 2. Log In
1. Go to the [Web Player](https://rbtm0106.github.io/fromm-jaime-vraiment-pas/) and log in with your Fromm account (credentials stay stored locally in your browser).

> **Note on sessions:** Logging in here might log you out of your phone app the first time. However, once you are logged in on this webpage, logging back into your phone app won't kick you out of the web player.

---

## Bonus: Convert `.ts` to `.mp4`

1. Download [`ts_to_mp4.zip`](https://github.com/rbtm0106/fromm-jaime-vraiment-pas/releases/latest/download/ts_to_mp4.zip) and unzip it.
2. Drag and drop your `.ts` file onto the `.bat` file.
* [Video Demo](https://youtu.be/jSEhjIaNUSs)
